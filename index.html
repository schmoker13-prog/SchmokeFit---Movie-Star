00. MOVIE STAR PROTOCOL
THE MINIMALIST GUIDE

Built on absolute efficiency, this protocol prioritizes heavy key lifts, optimal recovery, and flexible nutrition so you build a dense, powerful physique without living in the gym.

01. TRAINING BLUEPRINT

PHILOSOPHY: 3 intense workouts per week, training to or near failure on every set. Follow each session with a full day of rest. Maximum effort followed by maximum recovery.

REVERSE PYRAMID TRAINING (RPT):

  • Set 1 (Heavy): Target 4–6 reps (Heaviest Weight).
  • Set 2 (Medium): Drop weight ~10–20%, target 6–8 reps.
  • Set 3 (Light): Drop weight another ~10–20%, target 8–10 reps.
  • Rest Period: Rest 2.5 to 3 minutes between all sets.

⏱️ TIME CRUNCH RULE: If time is short, perform only the Key Lifts for that day and skip supplemental work.

02. CHOOSE YOUR FOCUS

The Movie Star Protocol keeps the original 3-day workout intact and makes only small Monday and Friday adjustments based on the area you want to emphasize most.

SHOULDERS: Broader delts and a stronger V-taper. This is the default program.

ARMS: More direct biceps and triceps work for added size and definition.

CHEST: More pressing and chest-focused volume for upper-body thickness.

LEGS: More quad, hamstring, and lower-body volume while keeping the upper body balanced.

Wednesday stays essentially the same across every focus. Your key lifts and overall workout length stay intact; only a small amount of Monday and Friday accessory work changes. You can switch focus anytime from the Workout tab.

03. THE 18-WEEK RESET

TRAIN IN BLOCKS: The Movie Star Protocol is designed as an 18-week training cycle rather than one workout plan you repeat forever.

After you complete Week 18, the app will prompt you to either keep your current training focus or choose a new focus: Shoulders, Arms, Chest, or Legs.

Once you make your selection, your program automatically resets to Week 1 for a fresh 18-week block. The core program stays familiar, while the focus option makes small changes to Monday and Friday so you can emphasize a different area without completely changing what works.

WHY RESET? It gives you a clear finish line, a chance to reassess your goals, and a simple way to refresh your training instead of running the exact same emphasis indefinitely.

03. NO-BS NUTRITION BLUEPRINT

Hitting your physique goals comes down to tracking two core metrics: Calories and Protein.

• Daily Calorie Target (Deficit): Bodyweight (lbs) × 13

• Daily Protein Target: Bodyweight (lbs) × 0.82g

*Note: Use your target bodyweight if you have significant weight to lose.

TRACKING & FLEXIBLE EATING:

  • Tracking App: Log everything using Cronometer or MyFitnessPal.
  • Food Scale: Recommended for weighing food precisely at home.
  • Flexible Nutrition: You don't need 100% "clean" meals. Fast food like McDonald's works as long as it fits your daily calorie and protein targets!
05. PHASE PROGRESSION

• Weeks 1–10 (The Fat Loss Deficit): Maintain a consistent caloric deficit while preserving dense muscle mass.

• 6–8 Weeks (The Recomp Phase): Add 200 calories to your daily intake every two weeks, slowly working into a slight surplus.

• Reset & Repeat: Recalculate using Bodyweight × 13 at your new lean weight and repeat as needed.

OFF DAYS & RECOVERY

Muscle is built during recovery. Take advantage of off days and weekends to rest fully. Do as little physical activity as possible on rest days.
